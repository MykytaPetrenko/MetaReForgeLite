In this section, you can import meshes from an FBX file.

You can export the head and body meshes from Unreal Engine as FBX files. To obtain a full-body mesh, your Metahuman should be undressed (to underwear, of course 😊).

This section also accepts LOD0 FBX files generated by the full version of MetaReForge. This means you can reuse Metahumans from your older MetaReForge projects as well.
